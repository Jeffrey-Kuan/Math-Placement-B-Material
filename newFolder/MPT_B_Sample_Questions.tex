\section*{Math Placement B-Test Sample Problems}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Perform the indicated operations and reduce the answer to lowest terms.

\begin{enumerate}
\item $\left(\frac{x^{3}+x}{x^{2}+x}\right) \cdot\left(\frac{x^{2}-1}{x^{2}+1}\right)$
\item $\frac{x}{x+1}+\frac{1}{x-1}+1$
\item $\frac{3-\frac{1}{x}}{9-\frac{1}{x^{2}}}$
\end{enumerate}

\item Express in simplified form with no negative exponents: $\left(x^{5} y^{-7}\right)\left(x^{-8} y^{9}\right)^{-1}$

\item Write the equation of the line containing the point $(1,2)$ and parallel to the line $2 x+4 y=1$.

\item Find the $x$-intercept, $y$-intercept, and the slope of the line $2 x+y=2$. Sketch the graph of $2 x+y=2$.

\item Solve the system of equations for $x$ and $y$ :

$$\begin{aligned}
x-2 y & =3 \\
-3 x+2 y & =7
\end{aligned}$$

\item Factor completely: $6 x^{2}+13 x+6$

\item Solve the following equations for $x$ 

\begin{enumerate}
\item $x^{2}-3 x=x+5$
\item $x^{4}-10 x^{2}+9=0$
\item $3-\frac{1}{x}=\frac{x}{1-x}$
\item $S=8 \pi x+2 \pi r^{2}$
\end{enumerate}

\item Sketch the graph of the function $y=3-3 x^{2}$. Label all intercepts.

\item  Write an equation to state the following: $a$ varies jointly with $b$ and the square root of $c$ and inversely with the cube of $d$.

\item The triangle ABC pictured below is a right, isosceles triangle. If the length of side AC is 3 , give the lengths of the other two sides and the measures of angle $A$ and angle $B$.

\begin{image}
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{right-triangle-ABC.jpg}
\xmalt{Right triangle $ABC$ with the right angle at vertex $C$, side $AC$ along the bottom, side $BC$ vertical on the right, and hypotenuse $AB$ connecting the bottom-left to the top-right}
\end{image}

\item The two triangles pictured below are similar. If the perimeter of the second is 19.5 , give the lengths of its three sides.

\begin{image}
\includegraphics[alt={Two similar triangles: triangle ABC with sides 1, 2.5, and 3, and larger triangle XYZ},width=\textwidth]{similar-triangles.jpg}
\xmalt{Two similar triangles. Triangle $ABC$ on the left has side $CA = 1$, side $AB = 2.5$, and side $CB = 3$. Triangle $XYZ$ on the right is larger with the same shape but no side lengths labeled. Vertices correspond: $X$ to $A$, $Y$ to $B$, $Z$ to $C$}
\end{image}

 \item A rectangular poster is three times as high as it is wide. If it contains a picture of 125 square inches framed by a margin of 4 inches at the top and bottom and 3 inches on each side, find the width of the poster.

 \item How many gallons of 40 percent alcohol solution must be mixed with 70 percent solution to obtain 30 gallons of a 52 percent alcohol solution?

\end{enumerate}
